Which X API to use for which job

Posting, likes, DMs, your own account's data
Official X API. Third-party read APIs do not write, and owned reads are only $0.001 each.
Keyword search and monitoring
AnyAPI twitter.search pinned to its cheapest source, about 5.6× below twitterapi.io per post.
Profiles, followers, single posts by ID
twitterapi.io. Profiles in batches of 100+ cost 1 credit each, against $0.010 per profile on X.
Searches that often come back empty
Check the minimum charge. twitterapi.io bills 15 credits for an empty call; X bills per post returned.

twitterapi.io and AnyAPI are not run or endorsed by X, and X's terms restrict collecting data outside its API. Check both sets of terms against your use case.

X API pricing questions

Q.001 / Is the X API free?

Not for new developers. Since February 6, 2026, pay-per-use is the default and new sign-ups get no free tier. X gives $20 in credits after your first purchase.

Q.002 / How much does the X API cost per month?

There is no monthly fee on pay-per-use; you pay per resource. Reading 300,000 posts a month costs $1,500 at $0.005 each. Pay-per-use is capped at 3 million post reads a month; more needs an Enterprise plan.

Q.003 / How much does twitterapi.io cost?

$0.15 per 1,000 returned posts (15 credits each, $1 = 100,000 credits), with a minimum of 15 credits per call even when nothing comes back. Profiles in batches of 100 or more cost 1 credit each.

Q.004 / Are third-party Twitter APIs allowed?

They are not run or endorsed by X, and X's terms restrict collecting data outside its API. Read their terms and X's against your use case; anything that posts, likes or sends DMs needs the official API.

Q.005 / What is the cheapest way to search X by keyword?

On our November 2026 prices, AnyAPI's twitter.search pinned to its cheapest source: $0.00054 per page of about 20 posts, or about $0.027 per 1,000. Its dearest source charges 28 times more, so turn failover off.

Q.006 / Where do these prices come from?

X's developer pricing page, twitterapi.io's pricing page, and AnyAPI's live per-source price API, checked on November 2, 2026. Run getcheapapi quote x.search to price your own query; it spends nothing. Routes are ranked by price only.
