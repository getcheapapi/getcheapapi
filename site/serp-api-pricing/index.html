Where a SERP API bill grows

Asking for 100 results
Google stopped serving 100 results per page in September 2025, so most APIs bill 100 results as 10 searches.
Search operators
DataForSEO multiplies the price by 5 for each operator such as site: or intitle:.
Live instead of queued
DataForSEO live costs 3.3× its standard queue. Use the queue when results can wait a few minutes.
Silent failover
AnyAPI retries a failed cheap source on a dearer one by default, up to 4× the price for Google search. Pin the source and turn failover off.
Plans you do not use up
A SerpApi plan bills the same whether you use 10% or 100% of it; prepaid credits only cost what you spend, until they expire.

SERP API pricing questions

Q.001 / What is the cheapest SERP API?

On published prices checked November 2, 2026, Searlo's web-search tier at $0.30 per 1,000, then AnyAPI's cheapest Google source at $0.50 and DataForSEO's standard queue at $0.60. Serper drops to $0.30 per 1,000 on its largest pack. Which is cheapest for you depends on volume, speed and the result fields you need.

Q.002 / How much does SerpApi cost?

Monthly plans from $25 for 1,000 searches ($25 per 1,000) to $3,750 for 1 million ($3.75 per 1,000), with a free plan of 250 searches a month. Failed, errored and cached searches are not counted; unused searches do not roll over.

Q.003 / Is there a free SERP API?

Free allowances, not free APIs: SerpApi 250 searches a month, Brave $5 of credit a month, Serper 2,500 queries once, Searlo 3,000 queries once, DataForSEO $1 of trial credit. Google's own Custom Search JSON API is closed to new customers.

Q.004 / Is Google's Custom Search JSON API still available?

Not for new customers. Google says existing customers have until January 1, 2027 to move to another solution. Until then it costs $5 per 1,000 queries after 100 free a day, up to 10,000 a day.

Q.005 / How much do 100 results per query cost?

Since Google stopped honoring 100 results per page in September 2025, most providers bill 100 results as 10 pages. On AnyAPI that is about $0.005 by paging, against $0.024 for its single-call top-100 endpoint.

Q.006 / Where do these prices come from?

Each provider's own pricing page, and AnyAPI's live per-source price API, checked on November 2, 2026. Run getcheapapi quote serp.google to price your own query across routes; it spends nothing. Providers are ranked by price only.
